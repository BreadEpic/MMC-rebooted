**Games, Unblocked, Forever.**

The whole of MMC:Rebooted in one zip. No wifi needed.

### How to play
1. Download **Reboot-1.zip** below.
2. Extract it.
3. Open **Run Me.html**. Keep it next to the **files** folder, that's where every game lives.

### What's inside
- 74 games from Gams (HTML5, Unity, Flash through Ruffle, retro)
- The Ampler Minecraft launcher with 10 offline clients. Precision Client (1.8.8, working multiplayer) is the default.
- Home: playtime stats, most played pie chart, pinned games, jump back in, searchable library
- 13 themes: Neon, Hacker, White, Vaporwave, Amber CRT, Deep Sea, Inferno, Sakura, Handheld, Galaxy, Bubblegum, Frost, Cyber
- Settings: open mode, popout bar, tab disguise with presets

Multiplayer servers need internet. Everything else runs offline.
